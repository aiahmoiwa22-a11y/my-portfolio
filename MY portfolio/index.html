About Me.

My name is Aiah Donald Moiwa, a passionate Software Engineering student, AI filmmaker, and digital creator with a strong interest in using technology to solve real-world problems and tell meaningful stories. I enjoy building software, designing user-friendly applications, and exploring emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence. My experience includes developing software projects, creating database systems, designing user interfaces, and working with programming languages and development tools to bring ideas to life. My goal is to build impactful technology, create inspiring content, and contribute to projects that make a lasting difference.
